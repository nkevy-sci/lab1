What's the cloud?

The cloud is a vast, global network of computers and network devices. When people say their data is in the cloud they usually mean it is stored on a computer or computers that they themselves don't own. Individuals and companies alike would rather pay for services than build their own systems for data storage or advanced computing. The companies who own and operate these large computer networks are called cloud service providers.

Why work in the cloud?

Cloud services make it easier to adjust software systems as demand changes. For example, a system administrator can add computers to a system when demand increases and remove them when demand falls. Also, applications can be placed closer to their end-users, which helps to improve response times.
