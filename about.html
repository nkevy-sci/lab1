About This Course

This course helps people experience cloud computing for themselves. It provides an introduction and a foundation for continued exploration.

Though it is intended primarily for people who are interested in developing software in the cloud, non-software professionals may also find it useful, especially if they collaborate with software developers or related teams.

Because this course is introductory, it is best suited to people who are new to cloud computing. This course does not require programming experience, but it does assume you have some of the basics down.

I wrote this course while pursuing my master's in Instructional Science and Technology at Cal State Monterey Bay

About the Author

Presently, I am attaining my master’s degree in Instructional Science and Technology. I am interested in learning science and information systems. I especially like learning about how technology can support performance improvement and data-driven organizational change.

My coursework includes analyzing performance needs, assessing opportunities for improvement, designing and developing solutions, managing change, and conducting statistical analysis to evaluate results. I hope to apply these skills to help people and organizations drive continuous improvement.

As a former software developer with a background in electrical engineering and computer science, I bring a technical perspective to this work. I believe that aligning technologies, such as cmi5 and cloud computing, with clear procedures can deepen our understanding of how people learn and how organizations change.
